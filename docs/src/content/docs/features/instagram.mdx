## Overview

Whatomate supports Instagram Direct Messaging through Meta's Instagram Messenger API. Connect your Instagram Business or Creator account to receive and respond to messages from your followers directly within the platform.

## Key Features

- **Receive Messages**: Get text messages, images, videos, and other media from Instagram users
- **Send Replies**: Respond to messages with text and media attachments
- **Story Replies**: View and respond to story replies and mentions
- **Reactions**: See and send reactions to messages
- **Unified Inbox**: Manage Instagram and WhatsApp conversations side-by-side
- **Agent Assignment**: Assign Instagram conversations to agents
- **Contact Management**: Track Instagram contacts separately or alongside WhatsApp

## Limitations Compared to WhatsApp

Instagram messaging has some important differences from WhatsApp that you should be aware of:

### 24-Hour Response Window

Unlike WhatsApp's template system, Instagram does not have approved message templates. You can only send messages within a **24-hour window** after the user's last message. After this window closes, you cannot initiate new conversations.

### No Templates

Instagram does not support pre-approved message templates. You cannot send marketing or transactional messages proactively.

### No Interactive Elements

Instagram messages don't support interactive buttons, lists, or quick replies that are available on WhatsApp.

### No Broadcast Campaigns

Since there are no templates and no proactive messaging, bulk broadcast campaigns are not available for Instagram.

## Contact Identification

Instagram contacts are identified by their **IGSID** (Instagram-Scoped ID), which is a unique identifier specific to your Instagram Business Account. This means:

- Each Instagram account has its own set of IGSIDs
- IGSIDs are different from Instagram usernames or user IDs
- The same person will have different IGSIDs for different business accounts they interact with

## Getting Started

To start using Instagram messaging:

1. [Set up your Instagram Business Account](/getting-started/instagram-setup)
2. Configure the webhook in Meta Developer Console
3. Add your Instagram account in Settings → Instagram Accounts
4. Test the connection
5. Start receiving messages!

## Chat Interface

Instagram contacts appear in the chat interface with a pink Instagram icon badge. You can filter the contact list to show only Instagram contacts using the channel filter tabs above the search bar.

The chat header shows the contact's Instagram profile name or username, along with their IGSID for reference.

## Best Practices

1. **Respond Quickly**: Remember the 24-hour window - prioritize responding to Instagram messages promptly
2. **Use Canned Responses**: Create canned responses for common Instagram queries
3. **Monitor Story Mentions**: Story mentions and replies often require quick responses
4. **Train Agents**: Ensure agents understand the differences between WhatsApp and Instagram messaging capabilities
